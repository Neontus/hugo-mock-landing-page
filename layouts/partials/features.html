Lightweight Form Factor 🏃💨

Google Chrome Extension form factor allows for fast computation times and ease-of-use, promoting accessibility to our users.

Automatic Review Page Redirection🤖↩️

When user is navigating to a product page, our extension will automatically give the URL to the associated reviews page in order to scrape the relevant product reviews.

Automatic GPT Summary➕➖

GPT is used to summarize the top reviews and provide a concise overview of the product with detailed pros and cons, making it easier for users to make informed decisions.
